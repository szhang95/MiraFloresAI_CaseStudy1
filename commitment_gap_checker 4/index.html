Commitment Gap Checker

Paste your meeting notes. It finds every promise made in the meeting and shows which ones still have no owner or no date, so you can fix them before everyone leaves. Runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

1. Add your meeting notes
My notes have no “Meeting date:” line

Type the day the meeting took place, so words like “Thursday” or “tomorrow” can become real dates. Format: 2026-10-27.

2. Check it

How it works

  1. Add meeting notes. Paste what was said in a meeting, or use the example.
  2. Click “Check for gaps”.
  3. Read the red box. It lists promises nobody owns, so you can assign them before the meeting ends.
